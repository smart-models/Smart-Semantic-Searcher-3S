## How to sign

In Your first pull request, add one line to
[`CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT-SIGNATURES.md`](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT-SIGNATURES.md)
with Your full name, Your GitHub account, Your employer if You contribute for
one, the date and the version of this Agreement. Do it in a pull request
opened from Your GitHub account, in a commit whose author email GitHub links
to that account, signed off with Your name (`git commit -s`). That commit is
Your signature. It covers that pull request, any Contribution You Submitted
before it, and Your later Contributions. You may end it for future
Contributions by telling Us in writing; Contributions already Submitted stay
covered.

If You contribute for Your employer, an authorised person at the employer may
confirm the authorisation required by Section 3(c) in a public issue with the
`licensing` label.

Questions about this Agreement go in a public issue with the `licensing`
label.
